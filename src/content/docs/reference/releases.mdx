SlideWorks has not had a numbered release yet. These notes list what is on the main branch, by feature.

## Foundation

- Decks built by an agent over MCP, from ready-made blocks, without writing HTML.
- A deck library, a deck page with the slide grid, speaker notes and an activity log.
- The player in the browser, on a laptop or a phone, with keyboard, click and swipe.
- Sharing: private, anyone with the link, or named people.
- Google sign-in, with sign-ups that wait for an admin's approval, and agent sign-in with a consent page.
- Single-container install with Docker Compose, SQLite storage and migrations on startup.

## Templates and palettes

- Three built-in templates: Talk, Workshop and Update.
- Four built-in palettes: Cobalt (the default), Ember, Plum and Midnight. Each palette passes a contrast check.
- A look is a template plus a palette. Change it from the deck's **Look** panel.
- 19 layouts, from the title and divider slides to charts, timelines and tables.
- A share card for each deck.

## Presenter view and phone remote

- A presenter window with the notes, a timer and an overview. The audience window follows it.
- A talk length for each deck, with the time left shown and a warning as it runs out.
- A phone remote: the current slide, the next slide and the notes, with a next button, for the deck's owner.

## Audience join

- A QR code and a six-digit join code for each deck, with a standing code that works for every live session.
- **Go live** starts a session, and guests who arrive early wait in a lobby.
- Guests join with no name and no account. A join slide shows the code, the QR code and the address.
- A join bar that puts a small QR code and the code in the corner of every slide, turned on from the presenter's live chip.
- A separate short domain for joining, if your server has one.

## Polls

- A poll slide with 2 to 8 options that guests answer on their phones.
- Pick one, or several picks where the poll needs them.
- Results show live, after the guest has answered, or after the poll closes.
- The presenter opens and closes the poll, reveals the counts, shows the correct answer, and starts a new round.
- Earlier rounds are kept, and agents can read their results.

## Coming soon

- Audience questions and reactions. They are not available yet.
- Uploaded templates and more palettes.

## Layout additions

- Poll and join slides, which read their content from the live session.
- Table, chart and timeline layouts, with status colours on the timeline bars.
- Inline blocks: callouts with a tone, chips, badges and a stamp on the title slide.
