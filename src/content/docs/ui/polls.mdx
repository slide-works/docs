A poll is a slide. When you reach it, the phones of everyone who joined show the question and the options. The bars on the big screen grow as answers come in.

Polls need the audience to have joined. See [Audience join](/ui/audience-join/).

## Adding a poll to a deck

A poll uses the `poll` layout, with one poll per slide.

- A **question** of up to 120 characters.
- **Two to eight options**, each up to 60 characters.

An agent adds the poll with `add_slide` and sets its options. Its other settings are set through `update_poll` by the agent that built the deck. The presenter window shows the settings but doesn't change them.

The settings are:

| Setting | Choices | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Pick | One, or up to a number you set | Whether each guest picks one option or several. |
| Results | Live, after answering, or after close | When the room sees the counts. See below. |
| Correct answer | Optional | Marks one option as right, to show after the poll closes. |
| Opens | When the presenter reaches the slide, or by hand | Whether the poll opens by itself or waits for **Open**. |

## Running a poll

A poll moves through three states: **Not open yet**, **Open** and **Closed**.

- By default, a poll **opens when you reach its slide**. If it opens by hand, press **Open poll** when you're ready.
- It **closes when you press Close poll, or move to another slide**.
- **Reveal results** shows the counts to the room while the poll is open, if the room can't see them yet.
- **Show answer** marks the correct option. It's available after the poll closes, if the poll has a correct answer.
- **New round** asks the same question again from zero, after the poll closes. Earlier rounds are kept and listed under **Earlier rounds** in the poll panel. The big screen shows only the current round.

In the presenter window, the keys are:

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| `P` | Open the poll, or close it if it's open |
| `R` | Reveal results |
| `A` | Show the answer |

New round has no key.

The phone remote has the same controls in a strip under the slide preview. If an action is refused, its message shows above the strip, and a dismiss button clears it.

Only the deck owner's signed-in sessions can run a poll. An agent with edit access can open and close a poll on a live deck and read its results. It can't reveal results, show the answer or start a new round.

## What guests see on their phones

Pick one: a tap sends the answer. Tap another option to change it while the poll is open.

Pick several: tap the options you want, then press **Send**. Use **Change my answer** to clear your picks and choose again.

The phone shows one of these states:

- **Still open** while the poll runs, with "Tap an answer to send it."
- **Answer sent**, once the server has the answer.
- **Not sent yet**, or **Your answer wasn't sent.** with the reason, if the connection drops or the answer is refused.
- **Poll closed.** A guest who didn't answer sees "You didn't send an answer for this one."
- **You joined after this poll closed.** for guests who arrived late.

When the poll has a correct answer and you show it, each phone says "You got it." if the guest picked it, or "Not this time." if not. Nothing is scored.

Each guest can send 30 answers a minute. A guest who changes their answer many times in a short time is asked to wait.

## What the room sees

The **results** setting decides when counts are visible.

- **Live** (the default): the room sees the counts while the poll is open.
- **After answering**: each guest sees the counts once they have answered. The room sees them when you reveal them or when the poll closes.
- **After close**: the room sees the counts when the poll closes.

Your presenter window always shows the counts. With the after-answering setting, a guest's phone shows no counts until that guest has answered.

On the big screen, the poll slide shows the question, a bar and a percentage for each option, the poll's state and the number of answers. When results are hidden, a chip says so. A correct answer is marked once you show it.

A guest who joins after the poll closes can't answer it.

## Privacy and counts

Polls keep totals only. Nobody can see who picked what, and the server never sends a guest counts they aren't allowed to see.
