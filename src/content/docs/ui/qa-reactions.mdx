:::caution[Not live yet]
Audience questions and reactions are designed but not finished. The guest and presenter screens are still placeholders in the current build, so you can't use them in a talk yet. The page below describes the planned behaviour. It may change before it ships.
:::

## What the audience gets

Guests on the phone get two tabs under the slide:

- **Slide** shows the current slide, with any poll under it.
- **Questions** lists the questions from the room, with the number of open questions on the tab.

Guests can ask a question, vote on questions they like, and send reactions.

## Asking and voting

- Asking opens a sheet with a text box. A question is anonymous by default.
- A guest can switch on **Show my name** to show a name with that one question. The name is free text.
- Guests can vote on questions to show support for them.
- The deck can turn names off for everyone.

## Reactions

Guests can send five fixed reactions: thumbs up, heart, laugh, wow and clap. On the big screen they float up the right edge. Other phones don't receive them. The presenter sees a count of them.

## The presenter's Questions panel

The presenter window has a Questions panel with four tabs: **Open**, **To review**, **Answered** and **Hidden**. For each question, the presenter can:

- **Show** it on the big screen, or take it off again.
- Mark it **Answered**.
- **Hide** it, so it no longer shows to guests.
- **Approve** it, when the deck waits for approval first.

Nothing reaches the big screen unless you show it.

## Showing a question on screen

With **Show**, one question appears on the big screen as a card over the current slide. A separate `qa` slide lists the top five questions.

A deck setting says where a shown question appears:

- **Any slide** (the default): the card sits over whatever slide is up.
- **Q&A slide**: the card appears only while a Q&A slide is up.

## Settings

These switches are set per deck, with the Questions panel's settings strip:

- **Questions**: on or off. On is the default for live sessions.
- **Review first**: new questions wait for your approval before guests see them.
- **Names**: whether guests may show a name.
- **Reactions**: on or off.

Questions and reactions can also be turned off for one slide. This is proposed and not yet confirmed: on that slide, guests would see "Questions are off on this slide," and the reaction row would go. The list and votes would stay.
