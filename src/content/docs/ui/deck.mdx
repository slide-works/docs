---
title: "Deck page"
description: "See every slide, its speaker notes, the look and the activity log of one deck."
---

Select a deck's title in the [library](/ui/library/) to open its deck page. The page is the place to look at a deck as a whole. Only the deck's owner can open it. Anyone else sees **Deck not found**, even if the deck is shared with them. They open the deck in the player instead.

## Header

The header shows:

- The deck's title and slide count.
- Who can open it: **Private**, **Anyone with the link**, or the number of named viewers, such as **3 named viewers**.
- **Share**. See [Sharing](/ui/sharing/).
- The **▶ Present** button. Its arrow opens three ways to present:
  - **Present** opens the deck in the player, in this tab.
  - **Presenter view** opens the audience window in a new window, and the presenter window in this tab. See [Presenter view](/ui/presenter-view/).
  - **Control from your phone** shows a QR code that opens the phone remote.

People you share the deck with don't get this page. They use the **▶ Present** link in their library, which opens the player.

## Slide grid

Every slide appears as a thumbnail, in order. Select a thumbnail to show its details on the right.

If the deck has no slides, the page says **No slides yet**. Ask a connected agent to add some.

## Slide details

The panel for the selected slide shows:

- **Slide**: its number.
- **Layout**: the kind of slide, such as `bullets` or `compare`.
- **Steps**: how many reveals the slide has.
- **Link**: a link that opens the deck at this slide.
- **Speaker notes**: the notes for the slide. A slide without notes says **No speaker notes.**

Speaker notes show only here, in the presenter view and on the phone remote. They never reach viewers, the audience window or the player.

## Look

The **Look** panel sets how the deck looks:

- **Template**, with a note on what it is for.
- **Palette**, which sets the colours. It shows a swatch and a contrast check. A template that locks its palette greys this out.
- **Chrome**: **Part tracker**, **Progress bar**, and **Footer** (**Author**, **Date** or **Off**).
- **Logo**.
- **Draft**: stamps the title slide and every footer.
- **Deep dives**: lists which slides are deep dives. It is read only, because deep dives are set per slide.
- **Share card**: the preview that link previews show for the deck.

Each change saves at once.

## Activity

The **Activity** list is the deck's change log, newest first. Each row says who made the change, what changed, when, and how:

- **You**, for a change you made in the browser.
- **AI** plus the agent's name, for a change an agent made.
- **via MCP** for a change made through an agent's tools, with the tool's name.
- **in the browser** for a change made on the site.

Sharing changes appear here too, with the agent's name when an agent made them.

## Missing decks

If the page says **Deck not found**, the deck was deleted, or it isn't yours to open. Choose **Back to your decks** to return to the library.
